How to Maximize Your DoorDash Earnings in 2025

Published April 14, 2025 | Updated April 14, 2025

DoorDash remains a top side hustle in 2025, with drivers earning $12-$25/hour after expenses. To help you make more, we’ve gathered strategies based on driver insights and 2025 trends.

1. Dash During Peak Times

High-demand periods offer better pay through base fares and promotions. In 2025, focus on:

  • Lunch Rush: 11 AM-2 PM, especially weekdays.
  • Dinner Rush: 5-8 PM, daily.
  • Weekends: Friday-Sunday evenings.

Tip: Schedule dashes in advance to secure high-demand slots.

2. Pick High-Value Orders

Not all orders are equal. Aim for deliveries with:

  • Higher payouts: $6+ for short distances.
  • Tip potential: Restaurants or grocery orders often tip better.
  • Efficiency: Avoid long waits (e.g., fast food during rushes).

Tip: Use the DoorDash app’s “Decline” feature to skip low-paying orders without penalty.

3. Optimize Your Route

Minimize mileage to save gas ($0.40-$0.60/mile). Strategies include:

  • Staying in busy zones like downtown or near malls.
  • Stacking orders when possible (multi-delivery).
  • Using navigation apps to avoid traffic.

4. Boost Tips with Great Service

Tips average $2-$5/order for courteous drivers. Enhance your service by:

  • Communicating clearly (e.g., texting arrival updates).
  • Ensuring orders are correct and well-packaged.
  • Delivering promptly, especially for hot food.

5. Take Advantage of Promotions

DoorDash offers bonuses like “Earn $10 extra for 5 deliveries.” Check for:

  • Peak Pay: $1-$4 extra per delivery.
  • Challenges: Weekly goals for bonus payouts.
  • Referrals: Earn $50-$200 for new dashers.
