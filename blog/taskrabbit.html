How to Start a TaskRabbit Side Hustle in 2025

Published April 14, 2025 | Updated April 14, 2025

TaskRabbit connects skilled workers with clients, offering a flexible side hustle in 2025. Taskers earn $20-$50/hour for jobs like furniture assembly or moving help. Here’s how to succeed.

1. Pick High-Demand Tasks

Popular tasks in 2025 include:

  • Furniture Assembly: $25-$40/hour.
  • Moving Help: $30-$50/hour.
  • Home Repairs: $20-$35/hour.

Tip: Check TaskRabbit’s app for trending tasks in your area.

2. Create a Strong Profile

Stand out with:

  • A professional photo.
  • A bio detailing skills (e.g., “IKEA assembly expert”).
  • Availability clearly listed.

Tip: Pass TaskRabbit’s background check to unlock more jobs.

3. Set Competitive Rates

Rates vary by city and task:

  • Urban Markets: $30-$50/hour.
  • Smaller Cities: $20-$35/hour.

Tip: Start 10% below average to attract initial clients, then raise rates.

4. Deliver Excellent Service

Earn 5-star reviews by:

  • Arriving on time with tools.
  • Communicating clearly.
  • Going the extra mile (e.g., cleaning up post-task).

Tip: Positive reviews boost your ranking on TaskRabbit’s algorithm.

5. Maximize Bookings

Work 10-20 hours/week to earn $800-$2,000/month. Update availability daily and accept quick tasks for steady income.
