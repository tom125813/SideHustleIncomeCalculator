How to Make Money on TikTok as a Side Hustle in 2025

Published April 14, 2025 | Updated April 14, 2025

TikTok continues to dominate social media in 2025, with creators earning $100-$1,500/month from small accounts (5k-20k followers). Here’s how to turn TikTok into a profitable side hustle.

1. Build a Niche Following

Focus on a specific niche to attract engaged followers:

  • Comedy: Skits, relatable humor.
  • Education: Quick tips (e.g., cooking hacks, coding).
  • Lifestyle: Fitness, fashion trends.

Tip: Post 1-3 videos daily to grow faster.

2. Optimize Your Content

Create viral-worthy videos with:

  • Trending sounds (check TikTok’s Creative Center).
  • Short, engaging clips (15-30 seconds).
  • Hashtags like #ForYou and #TikTokTips2025.

Tip: Use free editing apps like CapCut for professional effects.

3. Monetize Your Account

With 1,000+ followers, you can earn through:

  • Creator Fund: $0.02-$0.04 per 1,000 views.
  • Brand Deals: $50-$500/post (5k-20k followers).
  • Affiliate Links: Earn 5-15% commissions.

Tip: Join TikTok’s Creator Marketplace to connect with brands.

4. Engage Your Audience

Boost growth by:

  • Replying to comments to build community.
  • Going live (requires 1,000 followers).
  • Collaborating with other creators.

Tip: Lives can earn $50-$200/month via gifts from fans.

5. Scale Your Earnings

Creators with 10k followers can earn $500+/month. Spend 5-10 hours/week creating content to hit this goal within 3-6 months.
