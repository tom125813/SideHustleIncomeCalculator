How to Make Money with Rover in 2025

Published April 14, 2025 | Updated April 14, 2025

Rover connects pet lovers with owners, offering a flexible side hustle in 2025. Sitters and walkers earn $15-$40/hour depending on services and location. Here’s how to get started.

1. Choose Your Services

Rover offers multiple gigs:

  • Dog Walking: 30-minute walks ($10-$20).
  • Pet Sitting: Overnight care ($30-$60/night).
  • Drop-In Visits: Quick check-ins ($10-$15).

Tip: Combine services to maximize bookings.

2. Build a Winning Profile

Attract clients with:

  • Clear photos with pets.
  • A bio highlighting experience (e.g., “Caring for dogs 5+ years”).
  • Certifications like pet CPR (optional, boosts trust).

Tip: Complete Rover’s background check for credibility.

3. Set Competitive Rates

Check local rates on Rover’s platform. Average prices in 2025:

  • Urban Areas: $20-$40/service.
  • Suburbs: $15-$30/service.

Tip: Offer discounts for repeat clients to build loyalty.

4. Provide Top-Notch Service

Earn 5-star reviews by:

  • Sending photo updates to owners.
  • Following care instructions precisely.
  • Being punctual and responsive.

Tip: Reviews drive 80% of bookings, per Rover data.

5. Grow Your Client Base

Work 10-15 hours/week to earn $500-$1,500/month. Promote your profile on social media or ask clients for referrals.

Tip: Join local pet groups on social media to attract clients.

Final Thoughts

Rover in 2025 is perfect for animal lovers seeking flexibility. With great service and smart pricing, you can turn pet care into profit. Sign up today!
