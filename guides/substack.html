How to Make Money with Substack in 2025

Published April 14, 2025 | Updated April 14, 2025

Substack is a leading platform for writers in 2025, with creators earning $200-$2,000/month through paid newsletters. Here’s how to start and monetize your Substack side hustle.

1. Choose a Unique Niche

Stand out with a specific topic:

  • Personal Finance: Budgeting tips, investing.
  • Tech Trends: AI, gadgets.
  • Niche Hobbies: Gardening, board games.

Tip: Research popular Substack newsletters to find gaps in the market.

2. Set Up Your Newsletter

Create a professional Substack with:

  • A catchy name and logo.
  • A clear “About” page explaining your value.
  • Free content to attract initial subscribers.

Tip: Substack is free to start—no upfront costs!

3. Grow Your Subscriber Base

Attract readers by:

  • Posting 1-2 free posts/week.
  • Promoting on social media (Twitter/X, LinkedIn).
  • Collaborating with other Substack writers.

Tip: Aim for 500 free subscribers before launching paid tiers.

4. Monetize with Paid Subscriptions

Offer premium content:

  • Monthly Fee: $5-$10/month (Substack takes 10% + Stripe fees).
  • Exclusive Content: Deep dives, Q&As.
  • Annual Plans: Offer a discount to encourage long-term subscribers.

Tip: Convert 5-10% of free subscribers to paid for steady income.

5. Scale Your Earnings

With 100 paid subscribers at $5/month, you’ll earn $500/month. Spend 5-10 hours/week to reach this within 6 months.

Tip: Use analytics to see what content converts best and double down.

Final Thoughts

Substack in 2025 is a writer’s dream for building a side hustle. With a focused niche and consistent effort, you can turn your words into cash. Start your newsletter today!
