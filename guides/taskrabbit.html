How to Start a TaskRabbit Side Hustle in 2025

Published April 14, 2025 | Updated April 14, 2025

TaskRabbit connects taskers with clients needing help, offering a flexible side hustle in 2025. Taskers can earn $20-$50/hour depending on the task and location. Here’s how to get started.

1. Choose High-Demand Tasks

Popular tasks on TaskRabbit in 2025 include:

  • Furniture Assembly: $30-$60/hour.
  • Moving Help: $40-$70/hour.
  • Home Cleaning: $25-$50/hour.

Tip: Check TaskRabbit’s marketplace to see what’s in demand in your area.

2. Build a Strong Profile

Attract clients with:

  • A professional photo and detailed bio.
  • Skills and experience (e.g., “Expert in IKEA furniture assembly”).
  • Availability clearly listed.

Tip: Highlight any certifications, like handyman skills, to stand out.

3. Set Competitive Rates

Research local rates on TaskRabbit. Average prices in 2025:

  • Urban Areas: $30-$70/hour.
  • Suburbs: $20-$50/hour.

Tip: Start slightly lower to build reviews, then increase rates.

4. Deliver Excellent Service

Earn 5-star reviews by:

  • Being punctual and professional.
  • Communicating clearly with clients.
  • Going the extra mile (e.g., cleaning up after a task).

Tip: Reviews are critical—aim for 5 stars on your first 5 tasks.

5. Scale Your Earnings

Work 10-15 hours/week to earn $500-$1,500/month. Focus on repeat clients and high-paying tasks like moving or assembly.

Tip: Use TaskRabbit’s app to manage bookings efficiently.

Final Thoughts

TaskRabbit in 2025 offers flexibility for gig workers. With the right tasks and great service, you can build a profitable side hustle. Sign up today!
