How to Start an Online Tutoring Side Hustle in 2025

Published April 14, 2025 | Updated April 14, 2025

Online tutoring is a flexible side hustle in 2025, with tutors earning $15-$50/hour depending on expertise and platform. Here’s how to get started.

1. Choose Your Subject

Focus on high-demand areas:

  • STEM: Math, coding, science.
  • Languages: English, Spanish, Mandarin.
  • Test Prep: SAT, GRE, IELTS.

Tip: Pick a subject you’re passionate about and skilled in.

2. Select a Platform

Top platforms in 2025 include:

  • Preply: $15-$30/hour, flexible scheduling.
  • Chegg Tutors: $20-$40/hour, academic focus.
  • Wyzant: $25-$50/hour, local and online options.

Tip: Start on multiple platforms to find the best fit.

3. Build a Strong Profile

Attract students with:

  • A professional photo and bio.
  • Credentials (e.g., degrees, certifications).
  • A short intro video showcasing your teaching style.

Tip: Offer a discounted trial lesson to attract your first students.

4. Set Competitive Rates

Base your pricing on experience:

  • Beginners: $15-$25/hour.
  • Experienced Tutors: $30-$50/hour.

Tip: Adjust rates as you gain reviews and expertise.

5. Grow Your Client Base

Work 10-15 hours/week to earn $500-$1,500/month. Promote your services on social media or ask for referrals from satisfied students.

Tip: Create short educational videos on TikTok to attract students.

Final Thoughts

Online tutoring in 2025 is perfect for sharing knowledge while earning money. With the right platform and strategy, you can build a rewarding side hustle. Start teaching today!
