How Can I Make More Money in 2025?

Published April 14, 2025 | Updated April 14, 2025

Looking to boost your income in 2025? Side hustles are a great way to make extra money on your own schedule. Whether you’re driving, creating content, or freelancing, there’s a side hustle for everyone. Below, we’ll explore the best ways to make more money and help you calculate your potential earnings.

1. Explore High-Earning Side Hustles

Some side hustles pay better than others. Here are a few top options:

  • Driving with Uber: Earn $15-$35/hour in urban areas.
  • Freelancing on Upwork: Make $15-$50/hour with in-demand skills.
  • Selling on Etsy: Profit $5-$50 per item with handmade goods.

Tip: Check out our Top 10 Side Hustles for the highest earners.

2. Use Our Free Calculator

Not sure how much you can make? Our Side Hustle Calculator lets you estimate earnings for any side hustle. Just select your hustle, input your hours, and get a personalized estimate.

Tip: Try calculating for multiple hustles to find the best fit!

3. Start with Low-Barrier Options

Don’t have a car or special skills? Try these:

  • Instagram Monetization: Grow a following and earn with sponsorships.
  • Affiliate Marketing: Promote products and earn commissions.
  • Virtual Assistant Work: Provide remote support for $15-$40/hour.

Tip: Many side hustles require little to no upfront investment.
